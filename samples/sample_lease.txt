RESIDENTIAL LEASE AGREEMENT (SAMPLE — SYNTHETIC, NOT A REAL DOCUMENT)

This Lease Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into between Example Properties LLC
("Landlord") and Jordan A. Sample ("Tenant") for the property located at
123 Fictional Street, Springfield, ST 00000 ("Premises").

1. TERM. The initial term of this Agreement is twelve (12) months, beginning
January 1, 2026 and ending December 31, 2026. Unless Tenant provides written
notice of intent to vacate at least ninety (90) days before the end of the
term, this Agreement shall automatically renew for successive twelve (12)
month terms under the same conditions, including any rent adjustment
permitted under Section 2.

2. RENT. Tenant shall pay Landlord monthly rent of $1,800.00, due on the 1st
of each month. Landlord may increase rent upon any renewal term by up to 8%
with thirty (30) days written notice. A late fee of $150.00 plus $25.00 per
additional day shall apply to any payment received after the 3rd of the
month, with no stated maximum.

3. SECURITY DEPOSIT. Tenant shall pay a security deposit of $1,800.00 upon
signing. The deposit may be withheld, in whole or in part, for damages
beyond normal wear and tear, unpaid rent, or "any other reason Landlord
deems appropriate in its sole discretion." No specific timeline for deposit
return is stated in this Agreement.

4. MAINTENANCE. Tenant shall be responsible for all repairs and maintenance
to the Premises, including but not limited to plumbing, electrical systems,
and structural elements, regardless of the cause of the needed repair.

5. TERMINATION. Should Tenant need to terminate this Agreement before the
end of the term for any reason, Tenant shall owe Landlord the entirety of
the remaining rent due under the term as liquidated damages, due
immediately upon notice of early termination.

6. ASSIGNMENT AND SUBLETTING. Tenant may not sublet the Premises or assign
this Agreement under any circumstances without Landlord's prior written
consent, which Landlord may withhold for any reason or no reason.

7. DISPUTE RESOLUTION. Any dispute arising under this Agreement shall be
resolved exclusively through binding arbitration administered by Landlord's
chosen arbitration provider. Tenant waives any right to a jury trial or to
participate in a class action related to this Agreement.

8. INDEMNIFICATION. Tenant agrees to indemnify, defend, and hold harmless
Landlord from any and all claims, damages, or liabilities arising from
Tenant's use of the Premises. Landlord assumes no corresponding obligation
to Tenant.

9. DEFAULT. Landlord may declare Tenant in default for any violation of this
Agreement, as determined in Landlord's sole discretion, and may pursue all
remedies available including immediate termination.

10. GOVERNING LAW. This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of
Example.
